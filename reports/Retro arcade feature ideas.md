# Turn two solo games into a party

The most valuable next step for this project is not more content but a **social layer around what already exists**. The best value per hour comes from four cheap features: an arcade-style initials high-score table with game-over stats, a Wordle-style spoiler-free result share for the existing Daily seed (plus a matching Daily for Star Vanguard), challenge codes carried in the URL fragment, and quick-rematch/pass-the-phone party rules. Each is Low effort in plain JS with localStorage, and together they turn private best scores into something friends compare and argue about. After that, the biggest gameplay wins fill each game's one signature gap. Star Vanguard lacks Galaga's **tractor-beam capture and dual-fighter rescue**, the mechanic critics credit for Galaga's lasting appeal. The Tetris clone lacks **T-spin detection**, which every modern attack table depends on, so it must come before any versus mode. Medium-effort, high-impact items come next: local split-keyboard Tetris versus with garbage, two-ship co-op, and a heuristic AI opponent. Online P2P play, global hosted leaderboards and expert scoring systems (hidden rank, drain-gauge chains) cost the most and give a casual group of friends the least. One caveat applies throughout: the effort ratings below are engineering judgement for a single JS developer, and no source measures fun or retention for these specific features. The ranking is a reasoned inference from how successful games are built, not playtest data.

## A shared scoreboard beats new content for a friend group

High scores were the original social engine of arcades. Space Invaders drove repeat play through an ever-rising best score, Exidy's Star Fire (1978) let players save initials beside it, and "high scores are inherently competitive," fuelling one-upmanship and giving Twin Galaxies as much press coverage as any other game topic from 1982 to 1985 ([Wikipedia: High score](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_score)). Eurogamer singled out leaderboards as adding to Galaga's "addictiveness" ([Wikipedia: Galaga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galaga)). For friends sharing one laptop or phone, a **top-10 table per mode with three-letter initials entry**, stored in localStorage and shown on the title screen, is probably the single best fun-per-effort feature in either game. It costs a few hours.

The table needs rules that keep it meaningful. Score chasers treat a continue as voiding a run ([shmups.system11.org](https://shmups.system11.org/viewtopic.php?p=1430994)), while the 1CC ("one credit clear") is now "a benchmark without being a barrier" for players on free play ([AUTOMATON](https://automaton-media.com/en/column/20220627-13597/)). Star Vanguard should therefore offer a continue countdown that tags the resulting score with a "C", give no-continue runs a **1CC badge**, and mark practice, cheat and assist-difficulty scores as unranked. Galaga's **hit-miss ratio** screen ([Wikipedia mirror](https://wikipedia2007.classicistranieri.com/g/a/l/Galaga.html)) is the template for a results screen. Add shots fired, accuracy, max combo, grazes and "x points from your best", and the game-over screen becomes a bragging card rather than a dead end.

### Daily seeds plus a pasteable result create a shared puzzle

The Tetris clone already has a date-seeded Daily, which is the infrastructure behind one of the most successful social mechanics of the decade. Wordle's share grid started with players posting emoji results by hand. It shows the outcome without the answer, so it pastes into any group chat spoiler-free, and one shared puzzle a day builds community ([Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/39248)). Wordle brought **"tens of millions"** of new users to the New York Times ([AOL/TechCrunch](https://www.aol.com/news/wordle-brought-tens-millions-users-150000203.html)). Developers of Caveblazers, Dead Cells and OlliOlli describe daily runs as a shared-seed puzzle with a "perfect line". Dead Cells sees **about 7,000 daily runs a day**, and one developer called the daily mode among the easiest big features to build ([Game Developer: The 24-hour ticket](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-24-hour-ticket-examining-daily-runs-)).

The missing piece is the share: a short text block such as "RetroTetris Daily #123 — 48,200 pts, 112 lines" plus a small emoji chart, sent through `navigator.share()` with a clipboard fallback. Star Vanguard should get the same treatment. Seed wave order, dive choices and drops from a date-keyed PRNG, and keep a separate unseeded RNG for cosmetic effects. Use the UTC date so friends in different time zones share a seed. The attempt rule matters. Spelunky's one-attempt daily disables unfair random elements ([Spelunky Wiki](https://spelunky.fandom.com/wiki/Daily_Challenge_Mode_(HD))), but critics argue single attempts distort how people play ([The Ludite](https://theludite.com/2014/06/23/spelunky-when-scoring-goes-bad/)). Dead Cells' unlimited attempts are "more welcoming" ([Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-24-hour-ticket-examining-daily-runs-)). For a casual group, the OlliOlli compromise fits best: unlimited practice, with the first attempt recorded separately. A weekly seed with a rotating modifier (no-hold, one-life, double speed) is a nearly free extension.

### Challenge codes give a friends leaderboard with no server

Without a backend, a **challenge link** is the cheap substitute for an online board. Put base64url of `{game, mode, seed, score, initials, version}` in the URL fragment so nothing is sent to a host, and keep it under about 200 characters. Long links reportedly stop being clickable in WhatsApp mobile, and the common compatibility guidance is under about 2,000 characters ([Glide forum](https://community.glideapps.com/t/share-link-to-current-screen-links-are-too-long/35968); [nuqs](https://nuqs.dev/docs/limits)). When a friend opens a code, store it locally as a "rival" entry. Over time this builds a friends leaderboard on each device. Be honest about the limits: anyone reading the JS can forge a score, and even Firebase App Check "does not guarantee the elimination of all abuse" ([Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/docs/app-check)). A checksum deters casual editing only, so frame the feature as rivalry among people who trust each other.

Hosted free leaderboards are the wrong default for this audience. dreamlo needs a small donation for HTTPS, is trivially fakeable and has had multi-day outages ([Unity forum](https://forum.unity.com/threads/dreamlo-not-working-with-webgl.634462/)). Firebase's free tier stops serving for everyone once the daily read quota is spent ([back4app](https://blog.back4app.com/firestore-pricing/)). A global board also collects names and IPs at a third party, which adds a Play Store data-safety obligation that local and share-code boards avoid entirely.

## Star Vanguard needs Galaga's signature risk and legible scoring

Star Vanguard already covers most of Galaga's structure. Its biggest gap is the **tractor beam**. In Galaga, a two-hit Boss Galaga can capture the player's ship at the cost of a life. Shooting the captor while it dives frees the ship, which docks as a **dual fighter with double firepower and a doubled hitbox**. Shooting the captor while it sits in formation turns the captured ship hostile instead ([Wikipedia: Galaga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galaga)). Critics single out the dual-fighter strategy and the bonus stages as what keeps the game engaging, and guides advise bringing a dual fighter into challenging stages to perfect them ([Wikipedia: Galaga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galaga); [Knoef guide](https://knoef.info/trophy-guides/ps4-guides/arcade-game-series-galaga-trophy-guide/)). It is the purest group-play decision in the genre: "do I let it take me?" Building it means a boss hover-and-beam state, a captive entity that rides in formation, rescue logic and a two-ship player. The existing wingman code covers much of the docking work, so the cost is **Medium**.

Several Galaga details cost almost nothing. Galaga awards extends at **20,000, 70,000 and every 70,000** on its default 3-life setting ([Museum of the Game](https://arcade-museum.com/tech-center/game-dips/galaga)). Score-based extends, scaled to Star Vanguard's score economy, give a steady mid-run reward. Its challenging stages pay a bonus for each wiped group of 8 and **10,000 for all 40** ([StrategyWiki](https://strategywiki.org/wiki/Galaga/Gameplay), single source). Star Vanguard already has the PERFECT bonus, so the additions are per-group payouts and a pattern that changes each loop. Mid-stage **enemy transforms** that spawn a trio with a group bonus ([Wikipedia: Galaga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galaga)) are Low to Medium effort and keep later loops surprising.

### Visible, forgiving scoring hooks suit casual players; hidden systems do not

Scoring adds depth beyond survival, but shmup veterans warn that chain systems can be "disproportionately punishing" unless capped and that complex rank is "really hard to get right" for newer players ([shmups.system11.org](https://shmups.system11.org/viewtopic.php?p=1571991)). The systems that fit a friend group are the ones a player can read at a glance. **Grazing**, scoring for near-misses, dates back to Raiden Fighters (1996) and is central to Touhou ([shmups.wiki: History](https://shmups.wiki/library/History)). It needs one extra distance check per bullet, and a graze meter that recharges the nova bomb ties it into existing systems. Its value is limited by how sparsely Galaga-style enemies fire. Battle Garegga's **medal ladder**, in which each catch raises the next medal's value from 100 toward 10,000 and a missed medal resets it ([Wikipedia: Battle Garegga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_Garegga)), creates "dive for it" moments. Ikaruga's **three-of-a-kind chain**, worth 100 and doubling to a 25,600 cap ([Wikipedia: Ikaruga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ikaruga)), maps naturally onto Galaga's three enemy types without needing polarity. Each is Low effort. Pick one, not all, because Star Vanguard already has a combo multiplier and stacking systems muddies it. A point-blank kill bonus and turning bullets into score pickups when the boss dies are cheap extras that reward the aggressive play groups enjoy.

The systems to avoid are expert tools. Garegga's hidden rank pushes players to stay powered down and to die deliberately ([Wikipedia: Battle Garegga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_Garegga)). DoDonPachi's draining combo gauge gates its second loop behind a 270+ hit chain ([Wikipedia: DoDonPachi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DoDonPachi)). Ikaruga's novel core loop was a barrier for newcomers ([Wikipedia: Ikaruga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ikaruga)). If Star Vanguard wants dynamic difficulty, the friendly version is a visible "HEAT" meter. It rises with fast kills and no-death time, speeds dives slightly, multiplies score, drops on death and never punishes power-up pickups. Zanac's ALC shows the idea ([Giant Bomb](https://www.giantbomb.com/zanac/3030-4394/)) without Garegga's perversity.

Two Touhou conventions make deaths feel fair for mixed-skill groups. A **focus/slow key with a visible hitbox** smaller than the sprite is cheap. So is a **deathbomb window**: a bomb pressed within about 8 frames of being hit cancels the death ([Wikipedia: Touhou Project](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Touhou_Project)). Both are Low effort and reuse existing systems. At 240x320 the hitbox dot should be 1 to 2 px with an outline. Gradius-style trailing options ([Wikipedia: Gradius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gradius)) or a charge shot are Medium-effort perks for a ship class. Boss phases with a no-hit, no-bomb capture bonus give clear moment-to-moment goals, at the Medium cost of authoring the patterns.

## T-spin detection unlocks the Tetris clone's versus future

The Tetris clone already has the hard engine parts: SRS, the 7-bag, B2B and combos. What it lacks is the move that powers modern competitive Tetris. The Guideline rule takes about 40 lines of code. The T's last successful move must be a rotation, and at least **3 of the 4 diagonal corners** around its center must be occupied, with walls and floor counting as filled. Both front corners filled makes a full T-spin. Only one makes a Mini, unless the rotation used **SRS kick test 5** (1 across, 2 down), which upgrades it to full ([TetrisWiki: T-Spin](https://tetris.wiki/T-Spin)). Scoring is a level-multiplied lookup: TSS 800, TSD 1200, TST 1600, Mini 100 to 400, ×1.5 for back-to-back. Perfect-clear bonuses run from 800 to 2,000, and 3,200 for a B2B PC Tetris ([TetrisWiki: Scoring](https://tetris.wiki/Scoring)). Because the clone supports custom polyominoes, TETR.IO's **all-spin "immobile" check** (the piece cannot move left, right or up) is the natural generalisation for non-T pieces ([TetrisWiki: TETR.IO](https://tetris.wiki/TETR.IO)). Flashy "T-SPIN DOUBLE" and "PERFECT CLEAR" callouts give casual players a shared "did you see that" moment.

This matters for social play because **T-spins are the main attack source in every versus table**. The general Guideline table sends Double 1, Triple 2, Tetris 4, TSS 2, TSD 4, TST 6 and Perfect Clear 10 ([TetrisWiki: Garbage](https://tetris.wiki/Garbage)). Jstris adds a simple integer combo bonus of +1 to +5 and a +1 B2B bonus ([TetrisWiki: Jstris](https://tetris.wiki/Jstris)). A versus mode without T-spins would feel shallow to anyone who has played a modern client.

### Handling settings and training modes are cheap parity features

Fixed DAS/ARR is the clone's most likely objection from experienced players, and the fix is a settings panel. The Guideline recommends **DAS 10 frames (~167 ms) and ARR 2 frames (~33 ms)** ([TetrisWiki: Tetris Guideline](https://tetris.wiki/Tetris_Guideline)). TETR.IO adds SDF (soft-drop speed, up to 40× or instant) and DCD, and the community advises ARR 0 ([gamertagmythras](https://gamertagmythras.com/blog/tetris/tetris-tetrio-handling-settings-guide)). Jstris exposes these values in milliseconds, the natural unit for a browser game. Add IRS/IHS and a "prevent accidental hard drop" delay, and store everything beside the existing key remaps. Effort is Low.

Jstris's mode list is the community's de facto checklist: Sprint at several lengths, **Cheese race**, Survival (1 garbage line per second), Ultra, **PC Mode** (fail if no perfect clear within 10 pieces) and **20TSD** ([TetrisWiki: Jstris](https://tetris.wiki/Jstris)). Once garbage generation and T-spin detection exist, each is a Low-effort parameter on existing modes. Live **PPS, APM and KPP** plus Sprint splits against your personal best are Low effort and give friends numbers to compare ([TETR.IO wiki: Terms](https://tetrio.wiki.gg/wiki/Terms)). Finesse fault counting, as in TETR.IO's Pro modes ([TetrisWiki: TETR.IO](https://tetris.wiki/TETR.IO)), is Medium effort. It needs an optimal-input table built offline by BFS ([Hard Drop wiki: Finesse](https://harddrop.com/wiki/Finesse)) and appeals mostly to hardcore players. A TGM-style 20G Master mode with grades ([TetrisWiki: TGM](https://tetris.wiki/Tetris_The_Grand_Master)) is a Medium-effort niche.

## Local play delivers the social energy; online P2P is a later bet

Alternating two-player turns are trivial to add but historically weak. Galaga's 2P mode was unpopular because a strong first player could finish the game before the second player's first turn ([Wikipedia: Galaga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galaga)). It still suits pass-and-play on one phone if turns swap on every lost life, or per stage or per 40 lines. Better still is a **party mode** in which 2 to 8 named players each play the same seed in turn while a ranking table updates. It works on one phone in both games, and the effort is Low.

Real-time formats carry the energy. **Side-by-side Tetris versus** means refactoring the board into an instantiable object, sharing a seed so both players get the same bag, and adding a pending-garbage queue that the defender's own clears cancel 1:1 before it rises ([TetrisWiki: Garbage](https://tetris.wiki/Garbage)). Add a red pending meter and TETR.IO-style messiness ([TetrisWiki: TETR.IO](https://tetris.wiki/TETR.IO)) and it is Medium effort with very high casual value. A **heuristic AI opponent**, using a board evaluation weighted on holes, bumpiness and height with its pieces-per-second throttled to set difficulty, covers solo sessions at Medium effort. The stronger Cold Clear bot is MPL-2.0 and was archived in January 2024 ([GitHub: cold-clear](https://github.com/MinusKelvin/cold-clear)), so embedding it is a riskier, High-effort path. **Two-ship co-op** in Star Vanguard is also Medium, and the capture mechanic becomes a social hook: either player can rescue a captured partner. TETR.IO-style named "mods" double as handicaps without stigma: garbage multiplier, no-hold or starting gravity for Tetris, extra lives or a shield for the shooter. Add quick rematch with a first-to-3 tally and post-match "awards" such as Garbage King and Clutch Survivor ([TetrisWiki: TETR.IO](https://tetris.wiki/TETR.IO)). These are Low-effort UI and localStorage work.

Input is the practical constraint. Gamepads are Baseline-supported since 2017, but a pad appears only after a button press, must be polled each frame and requires HTTPS ([MDN: Gamepad API](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Gamepad_API/Using_the_Gamepad_API)). A "press any button to join" lobby solves both the exposure rule and player assignment. Gamepad behaviour inside Android System WebView is unconfirmed, so test it on a device before promising support. Split-keyboard play works, but cheap keyboards may drop simultaneous key presses. That is untested and should be checked.

Online play without a server is possible but costly. WebRTC data channels still need signalling. The options are the free PeerJS cloud broker ([PeerJS](https://peerjs.com/docs/)), which has no published SLA, or copy-paste SDP codes, which are clumsy and leak IPs ([serverless-webrtc-testbench](https://github.com/fcivaner/serverless-webrtc-testbench)). An estimated **10–25% of sessions need a TURN relay**, which costs money, and more on mobile carrier NAT ([Twilio](https://twilio.com/en-us/stun-turn); [Getstream](https://getstream.io/resources/projects/webrtc/advanced/stun-turn/)). Online Tetris versus is the best candidate because garbage already arrives with a delay. Each client runs its own board and exchanges only events, which is Medium effort. An online co-op shooter needs lockstep determinism or rollback, which is High effort ([Infil](https://words.infil.net/w02-netcode-p5.html)).

Ghost replays share that determinism dependency. They require a fixed-timestep simulation driven only by a seed and an input log. Tetris is integer-grid and close to deterministic already. Star Vanguard's dive curves are at risk because `Math.sin`/`cos` precision varies across engines ([Rune blog](https://developers.rune.ai/blog/making-js-deterministic-for-fun-and-glory/)). Precomputed path tables or `Math.fround` are the fix. A full replay is too long for a URL, so share it as a file or show the ghost only for your own best run. The same determinism work also gives you a real **attract mode** that replays a recorded good run ([Law of Game Design](https://lawofgamedesign.com/2016/07/29/phalanx3ds-attract-mode/)), so it pays off twice.

## Polish multiplies everything above, within pixel-art limits

"Juice it or lose it" demonstrated that tweening, squash and stretch, and small noises turn a plain Breakout into a satisfying one. Its authors stressed the tips are "very simple to implement" and that interactivity matters more than graphical fidelity ([Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/video-is-your-game-juicy-enough-)). A recreation of Vlambeer's "Art of Screenshake" lists the layers in order: muzzle flash, impact effect, hit flash, knockback, permanence (debris that stays), screen shake, recoil and hit pause ([DK Liao devlog](https://dkliao.itch.io/the-art-of-screenshake-recreation/devlog/451576/quick-breakdown-of-all-the-effects)). Most are Low effort in Canvas. Two constraints apply.

First, **hit-stop should be reserved for big events**: player death, boss kill, a Tetris or T-spin clear. Sakurai warns that freezing on every hit slows the pace ([Source Gaming](https://sourcegaming.info/2015/11/11/thoughts-on-hitstop-sakurais-famitsu-column-vol-490-1/)), and a shmup has a lot of hits.

Second, the 240x320 renderer demands that **every shake, kick and tween be rounded to whole pixels at draw time**. Sub-pixel offsets shimmer ([bugnet](https://bugnet.io/blog/how-to-fix-pixel-perfect-camera-jitter)). Use trauma-based shake (trauma² × smooth noise) applied as translation only ([Bevy example](https://bevy.org/examples/camera/2d-screen-shake/)), capped at 2 to 4 game pixels. Pool particles to avoid garbage-collection hitches on low-end WebViews. The existing vibration plugin is a free haptics channel.

Touch controls decide whether Star Vanguard works on the Android build at all. Forum consensus favours **relative-drag** movement, as in Cave's DoDonPachi ports, with a sensitivity multiplier and auto-fire, because direct tracking hides the ship under the finger ([TouchArcade](https://toucharcade.com/community/threads/preferred-control-method-for-shmup-bullethell-free-flight-games.125655/)). Players praise a 2× sensitivity option and a thumb zone outside the playfield ([TouchArcade](https://toucharcade.com/community/threads/relative-touch-control-for-shmups-done-right-no-finger-of-gameplay.287378/)). Integer-scaling the canvas leaves a letterbox on tall phones that can serve as that zone. The evidence here is forum consensus, not measured study, so offer two or three schemes and remember the choice.

Accessibility options double as casual-friendliness. The Game Accessibility Guidelines' Basic tier asks for a choice of difficulty, a game-speed option, no information carried by colour alone, remappable controls and no flicker ([gameaccessibilityguidelines.com](https://gameaccessibilityguidelines.com/full-list/)). Xbox guidance asks for a way to turn camera shake off ([XAG 117](https://devdocs.xbox.com/gaming/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/117)), and WCAG caps flashing at **three per second** ([W3C](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/three-flashes-or-below-threshold.html)). That means a shake slider, a reduce-flashing toggle and per-piece pattern overlays for Tetris, as Tetris Effect: Connected added ([Nintendo Everything](https://nintendoeverything.com/tetris-effect-connected-update-out-now-version-1-3-1-patch-notes/)). These should never be locked behind progression, which AbleGamers criticised Puyo Puyo Tetris for doing ([AbleGamers](https://ablegamers.org/unlockingaccessibilitypuzzlegames/)). Pause on `visibilitychange` and suspend the AudioContext when the WebView app is backgrounded.

### Achievements and unlocks should add variety, not power

The evidence on achievements is mixed. A field study found badges raised user activity ([Hamari 2017](https://research.utu.fi/converis/portal/detail/Publication/27336812?lang=en_US)). A lab study found no strong link to intrinsic motivation ([UTas](https://figshare.utas.edu.au/articles/journal_contribution/The_effects_of_videogame_achievements_on_player_motivation/22922864)). Some frequent gamers describe achievements as burdens, while others say they "encourage diverse ways to play" ([arXiv 2205.15163](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2205.15163)). The design implication is a small local set of 20 to 40 **skill feats and discoveries**, such as rescuing a dual fighter, a 100% hit ratio on a challenging stage, a T-spin triple or a perfect clear, with no grind counters. Unlocks follow the same logic. Players resent permanent power upgrades because they undermine die-and-retry ([Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1145350/discussions/0/4358999171576511867)) but welcome sidegrades and content ([feddit thread](https://feddit.it/post/5081108)). NES-style palette swaps, a sound test for the chiptune tracks, Boss Rush or Endless modes, and a Konami-code easter egg that flags its scores as unranked all fit. An Endless mode measurably raised perceived replayability in one shooter study ([HAW Hamburg](https://reposit.haw-hamburg.de/handle/20.500.12738/19142?mode=full)). Finally, follow Super Meat Boy's lesson to cut the penalty for dying to near-instant restart time ([Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/-i-super-meat-boy-i-s-mcmillen-explains-why-so-hard-)): one tap from game over to a new run.

## Conclusion

The project's real constraint is not "no backend" but "no shared space". For a friend group, the missing ingredient is the moment of comparison: an initials table on the shared device, a result pasted into the group chat, a code that says "beat this". Every one of those is achievable client-side, and together they cost less than a single new stage. A second-order point follows from the notes. Determinism is the hidden keystone. One fixed-timestep, seeded, trig-safe simulation simultaneously enables Star Vanguard dailies, ghost replays, a real attract mode, replay-verified challenges and any future online play. Making the engine deterministic early, even before building those features, converts several Medium-to-High items into cheap follow-ups.

The other insight is about ordering within each game. Galaga's capture mechanic and Tetris's T-spin are not just more features. They are each game's source of meaningful risk-taking, and versus or co-op modes built without them would inherit a shallow core. Fix the core verb first, then layer people on top of it. Expert scoring systems and online infrastructure answer the questions a hardcore community asks. A group of friends asks simpler questions, "who's winning?" and "one more go?", and the cheapest features above answer both.
